Facilitate smooth transitions

Use presets for common activities like think-pair-share or gallery walks. The timer keeps running even if you click away within the tab, and your last custom duration is remembered locally.

Teacher tips

  • Project the timer and narrate expectations at the start of each countdown.
  • Use the progress ring as a quick visual prompt for students who need reminders.
  • Toggle the chime off for silent work sessions or testing environments.
